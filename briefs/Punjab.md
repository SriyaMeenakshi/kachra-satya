_Data only from the Kachra Satya dataset. The CPCB and MoHUA figures are for different years; no same-year pair exists yet. Anything not in the data is marked 'not reported'._

**Gap: +51.6 percentage points** (MoHUA minus CPCB).

## 2. Likely causes (hypotheses from the data)

- **Different denominators.** Generated waste is 4,860 tpd in CPCB 2023-24 and 4,035 tpd in MoHUA 2025-26 (-17%). A percentage built on a different base will differ even if treatment were identical. Hypothesis: the state revised its generation estimate.
- **Treatment reported much higher in the newer source.** Treated/processed rose from 2,111 tpd to 3,833 tpd. Hypothesis: real capacity added between years, or a looser definition (MoHUA 'processed' is stored as treated here and may not mean the same as CPCB 'treated').

## 3. Flags raised for this state

- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): generated: CPCB 2023-24=4860.04; MoHUA 2025-26=4035; change=-17.0%. CPCB and MoHUA generated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.
- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): treated: CPCB 2023-24=2110.74; MoHUA 2025-26=3833; change=+81.6%. CPCB and MoHUA treated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.

## 4. Draft SBM-U 2.0 ask

On the CPCB figures for 2023-24, about **2,749 tpd** of 4,860 tpd generated is not reported as treated. We request:

1. A joint CPCB-MoHUA-state reconciliation of generated and treated tonnage for the same financial year, so the two published figures can be explained.
2. Central assistance under SBM-U 2.0 (solid waste component) for processing capacity, sized to the reconciled untreated quantity.
3. Support for weighbridge-based data reporting by urban local bodies so future returns pass basic checks.
