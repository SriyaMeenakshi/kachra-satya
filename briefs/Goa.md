**Gap: +0.8 percentage points** (MoHUA minus CPCB).

## 2. Likely causes (hypotheses from the data)

- **Different denominators.** Generated waste is 194 tpd in CPCB 2023-24 and 132 tpd in MoHUA 2025-26 (-32%). A percentage built on a different base will differ even if treatment were identical. Hypothesis: the state revised its generation estimate.

## 3. Flags raised for this state

- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): generated: CPCB 2023-24=193.9; MoHUA 2025-26=132; change=-31.9%. CPCB and MoHUA generated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.
- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): treated: CPCB 2023-24=187.9; MoHUA 2025-26=129; change=-31.3%. CPCB and MoHUA treated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.

## 4. Draft SBM-U 2.0 ask

On the CPCB figures for 2023-24, about **6 tpd** of 194 tpd generated is not reported as treated. We request:

1. A joint CPCB-MoHUA-state reconciliation of generated and treated tonnage for the same financial year, so the two published figures can be explained.
2. Central assistance under SBM-U 2.0 (solid waste component) for processing capacity, sized to the reconciled untreated quantity.
3. Support for weighbridge-based data reporting by urban local bodies so future returns pass basic checks.

_Amount: not stated. The dataset has no state-wise allocation or utilisation figures for this state; add them from the Lok Sabha Q682 annexure before submitting._
