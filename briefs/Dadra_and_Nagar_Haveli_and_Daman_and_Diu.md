**Gap: +19.5 percentage points** (MoHUA minus CPCB).

## 2. Likely causes (hypotheses from the data)

- **Different denominators.** Generated waste is 269 tpd in CPCB 2023-24 and 104 tpd in MoHUA 2025-26 (-61%). A percentage built on a different base will differ even if treatment were identical. Hypothesis: the state revised its generation estimate.

## 3. Flags raised for this state

- `2_collected_gt_generated` (2019-20): collected=191; generated=184; source=CPCB. More waste is reported collected than generated, which is not possible.
- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): generated: CPCB 2023-24=269.02; MoHUA 2025-26=104; change=-61.3%. CPCB and MoHUA generated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.
- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): treated: CPCB 2023-24=216.49; MoHUA 2025-26=104; change=-52.0%. CPCB and MoHUA treated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.

## 4. Draft SBM-U 2.0 ask

On the CPCB figures for 2023-24, about **53 tpd** of 269 tpd generated is not reported as treated. We request:

1. A joint CPCB-MoHUA-state reconciliation of generated and treated tonnage for the same financial year, so the two published figures can be explained.
2. Central assistance under SBM-U 2.0 (solid waste component) for processing capacity, sized to the reconciled untreated quantity.
3. Support for weighbridge-based data reporting by urban local bodies so future returns pass basic checks.
