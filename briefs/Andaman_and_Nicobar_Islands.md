# Action Brief: Andaman and Nicobar Islands

_Data only from the TrashTally dataset. The CPCB and MoHUA figures are for different years; no same-year pair exists yet. Anything not in the data is marked 'not reported'._

## 1. The gap

| | CPCB 2023-24 | MoHUA 2025-26 |
|---|---|---|
| Generated | 77 tpd | 61 tpd |
| Collected | 77 tpd | not reported by MoHUA |
| Treated / processed | 72 tpd | 60 tpd |
| % of generated treated | 93.4% | 98.4% |
| Source | ls_au50_2025-12-01.pdf, p.3 | mohua_81pct_answer.pdf, p.3 |

**Gap: +4.9 percentage points** (MoHUA minus CPCB).

## 2. Likely causes (hypotheses from the data)

- **Different denominators.** Generated waste is 77 tpd in CPCB 2023-24 and 61 tpd in MoHUA 2025-26 (-21%). A percentage built on a different base will differ even if treatment were identical. Hypothesis: the state revised its generation estimate.

## 3. Flags raised for this state

- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): generated: CPCB 2023-24=77.3; MoHUA 2025-26=61; change=-21.1%. CPCB and MoHUA generated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.
- `3_sources_differ_gt_10pct` (2023-24 vs 2025-26): treated: CPCB 2023-24=72.22; MoHUA 2025-26=60; change=-16.9%. CPCB and MoHUA treated figures differ by more than 10%. These are different years (no same-year pair exists yet), so part of the gap may be real change.

## 4. Draft SBM-U 2.0 ask

On the CPCB figures for 2023-24, about **5 tpd** of 77 tpd generated is not reported as treated. We request:

1. A joint CPCB-MoHUA-state reconciliation of generated and treated tonnage for the same financial year, so the two published figures can be explained.
2. Central assistance under SBM-U 2.0 (solid waste component) for processing capacity, sized to the reconciled untreated quantity.
3. Support for weighbridge-based data reporting by urban local bodies so future returns pass basic checks.

**Funding position** (Lok Sabha Q682, 28 Nov 2024). The annexure prints no unit; the reply's other sums are in Rs crore.

- SWM allocation 2021-2026: 5.5. SWM funds utilised/released till 25.11.2024: 1.36 (24.7% of the SWM allocation).
- Total mission allocation: 8.6. Total funds utilised/released: 1.95.
- Read the ask against this: a low utilisation share means the constraint may be execution, not money.
